BIOJACKED

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Biojacked is an app for training, nutrition and coaching. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. "We" means the Biojacked team. "Coach" means a coach who uses Biojacked to work with members. Coaches are independent businesses, not Biojacked employees.

The short version

  • We collect what you type, log and upload so the app can work, and so your coach can coach you.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.
  • Your health and fitness information is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

What we collect

Account. Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.

Profile. Your name, main goal, starting and goal weight, and your privacy choices (for example, whether your name shows on the team feed or schedule). Coaches also add a business name, social handle, join code, call hours and the coaching plans they offer.

Health and fitness information you enter. Body weight, workouts and set-by-set logs, lifts, the physique goal you build (builds, focus muscles, height, measurements and body-fat estimates), weekly check-in answers, progress photos, your food diary and meal photos, water, steps you enter, and daily habits.

Messages and bookings. Messages between you and your coach, call bookings, and coaching applications.

Coach billing. If you're a coach on a paid Biojacked plan, Stripe processes your payment on the web. We receive your plan, status and renewal date from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

What stays on your phone. Reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device. Display settings like your finish and text size are stored on your device.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and we don't read Apple Health.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your program, food targets, rings, streaks, medals, charts and goal.
  • To connect you with your coach: check-ins, reviews, messages, calls and program changes.
  • To show your wins to your team, only in the ways you allow.
  • To send sign-in codes and answer support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who can see your information

Your coach can see your profile, goal, check-ins, photos, weights, workouts, lifts, food diary and messages. That's how coaching works.

Other members on the same team can see your first name and last initial on wins posted to the team feed (a finished session, a new best, a medal), on challenge leaderboards, and next to your call time on the schedule. You can turn each of these off in the app. Members never see your weight, photos, food or messages.

Service providers who process data for us under contract, only to run Biojacked:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in, and sends sign-in emails.
  • Stripe handles payments for coach plans, on the web.
  • Anthropic (Claude) estimates the nutrition of a meal when you use "Describe a meal". Only the text you type and the photo you add are sent, after you allow it. Nothing else about you is included.
  • Open Food Facts receives a product's barcode number when you scan one, to look up the food. No personal information is sent.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. To delete it, open Profile (members) or Settings (coaches) and tap Delete account. This removes your account, check-ins, photos, logs, bookings and messages. Copies in backups are erased within 30 days. Deleting a coach account cancels the coach's Biojacked plan and removes their business from the app.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app, export your food diary, change what other members see, and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Photos are stored in private storage and shown through links that expire. Database rules limit each person to the data they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account secure, since it's how you sign in.

Children

Biojacked isn't meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for transfers.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
