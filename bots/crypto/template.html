Prices are Coinbase Exchange daily candles (UTC days), from each coin's listing through the date at the top. The default fee of 0.6% per trade is close to Coinbase's taker fee for small accounts; set yours. Slippage and taxes aren't included, and an idle position earns nothing.

A backtest shows what a rule would have done, not what it will do. Settings tuned on this same history will look better here than they do live, so judge a rule by how it holds up across coins and periods without retuning. Nothing here places trades or is financial advice.
