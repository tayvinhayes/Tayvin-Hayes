Fair chances come from the sportsbook odds you enter, with the book's margin removed. Sharper books such as Pinnacle, Circa and FanDuel give the most reliable numbers, and the pick'em line should match the book's line exactly. The entry math treats picks as independent; picks from the same game aren't, which is why one pick per game is on by default. Pick'em apps also require players from at least two teams in an entry.

Stakes use a quarter of the Kelly amount, capped at 5% of your bankroll. This page doesn't place entries; automating entries breaks the apps' terms. Not affiliated with PrizePicks or any sportsbook.
